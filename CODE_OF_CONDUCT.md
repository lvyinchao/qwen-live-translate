# Code of conduct

We welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, patient and specific. Critique code and ideas, and accept constructive feedback. Give credit and respect privacy.

Harassment, hateful language, sexualized conduct, threats, doxxing, deliberate credential disclosure, impersonation and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable in issues, pull requests or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause a discussion, warn contributors, or restrict participation in proportion to the behavior. Reports are handled with discretion; do not post private evidence in public issues. Contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/lvyinchao), or use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/lvyinchao/timbradub/security/advisories/new) when a report involves a security risk.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
